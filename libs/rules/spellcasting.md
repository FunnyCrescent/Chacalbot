# Spellcasting Rules — D&D 5e (2014 PHB)

## Casting a Spell

Every spell has six characteristics: casting time, range, components, duration, school, and level.

### Casting Time
- **1 Action**: Most combat spells. Cast on your turn as your action.
- **1 Bonus Action**: Cast as a bonus action. **IMPORTANT**: If you cast a bonus action spell, you may only cast a cantrip (level 0) with your action on the same turn. You cannot cast two non-cantrip spells in one turn even with Action Surge.
- **1 Reaction**: Cast in response to a trigger (e.g., *shield*, *absorb elements*, *counterspell*).
- **1 Minute or more**: These spells take multiple turns. Each round, you use your action to continue casting. If you take damage while casting, make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 10 + damage taken) or the spell fails and the slot is wasted.
- **Ritual**: See Ritual Casting section below.

### Spell Ranges
- **Touch**: The caster must touch the target. Requires an attack roll if the spell calls for it.
- **Self**: The caster is the target/point of origin.
- **X feet**: The point of origin is within X feet from the caster. Spells with areas of effect (cone, cylinder, sphere, line) originate from this point.

## Spell Slots

Spells of level 1-9 require spell slots to cast (cantrips are level 0 and do not require slots).
- Each class provides a number of spell slots per spell level per character level (see PHB spell slot table).
- When you cast a spell, you expend one slot of that spell's level (or higher, see Upcasting).
- Spell slots are restored on a **Long Rest** (for most casters). Warlocks regain pact slots on a **Short Rest**.
- **Upcasting**: Casting a spell at a higher level than its base. The spell's effects improve if it has a "At Higher Levels" entry.

### Multiclass Spell Slots
When multiclassing with spellcasting classes, combine levels for slots using the Multiclass Spellcaster table (PHB p. 165). Some classes count at partial rate: Ranger/Paladin levels ÷ 2 (rounded down), Eldritch Knight/Arcane Trickster levels ÷ 3 (rounded down).

## Concentration

Many spells require concentration to maintain their ongoing effects.
- **One concentration spell at a time**. Casting a new concentration spell ends the previous one.
- **Concentration check**: If you take damage while concentrating, make a Constitution saving throw: **DC = 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher**. Success = maintain concentration. Failure = spell ends.
- **Multiple damage sources**: Each source triggers a separate concentration check.
- **Incapacitated or killed**: Concentration automatically ends.
- **Casting another concentration spell**: The original spell ends (you choose which to keep if both are concentration).
- **Environmental conditions**: DM may call for checks due to rough seas, strong wind, etc.

## Ritual Casting

Some spells have the **Ritual** tag. These can be cast as rituals:
- Casting time increases by 10 minutes (added to the normal casting time).
- **Does NOT expend a spell slot**. This is the primary benefit.
- You must have the spell **prepared** (or in your spellbook for wizards) to cast it as a ritual.
- The **Ritual Caster** feat allows any class to cast ritual spells from a ritual book.
- Bards and Clerics can cast ritual spells as rituals if they have them prepared. Wizards can cast rituals from their spellbook without preparing them.

## Spell Schools

The eight schools of magic:
1. **Abjuration**: Protective spells, dispelling, wards (*mage armor*, *shield*, *counterspell*, *dispel magic*).
2. **Conjuration**: Summoning creatures, creating objects, teleportation (*summon familiar*, *misty step*, *teleport*).
3. **Divination**: Revealing information, seeing the unseen (*identify*, *detect magic*, *scrying*, *true seeing*).
4. **Enchantment**: Affecting minds, compelling behavior (*charm person*, *hold person*, *dominate person*).
5. **Evocation**: Direct energy, blasting (*fireball*, *lightning bolt*, *magic missile*, *cure wounds*).
6. **Illusion**: Deceiving senses (*invisibility*, *mirror image*, *major image*, *phantasmal force*).
7. **Necromancy**: Manipulating life force, undeath (*animate dead*, *false life*, *chill touch*, *revivify*).
8. **Transmutation**: Altering form or properties (*polymorph*, *fly*, *haste*, *bless*).

## Spell Components

Every spell lists its components: V (verbal), S (somatic), M (material).

### Verbal (V)
- Most spells require a spoken incantation. **Silenced** creatures cannot cast V spells. **Gagged** creatures cannot cast V spells.

### Somatic (S)
- Requires at least one free hand to perform gestures. A creature with both hands full (e.g., holding weapon + shield) cannot cast S spells unless they have War Caster feat.
- **War Caster feat**: Allows somatic components even with hands full; advantage on concentration checks; can cast single-target spells as opportunity attacks.

### Material (M)
- Requires specific material components listed in the spell. A **spellcasting focus** (arcane focus, druidic focus, holy symbol) can replace non-costly, non-consumed materials.
- **Costly materials**: If the component has a gold cost (e.g., "a pearl worth at least 100 gp"), a focus CANNOT replace it — you must have the actual component.
- **Consumed materials**: If the component is consumed on casting (e.g., "a pinch of sulfur, consumed"), it must be provided each time.
- **Component pouch**: Contains tiny common items and replaces any non-costly M component.

## Counterspell and Dispel Magic

- **Counterspell** (3rd level, reaction): If a creature within 60 ft casts a spell of 3rd level or lower, it automatically fails. For higher levels, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability (DC = 10 + the spell's level). No attack roll or saving throw.
- **Dispel Magic** (3rd level, action): Ends one spell effect on a target (3rd level or lower auto-succeeds). For higher-level effects, ability check DC = 10 + spell level.

## Known vs. Prepared Spells

- **Bards, Rangers, Sorcerers, Warlocks**: Know a fixed number of spells. Cannot change them except by leveling (or swapping on level-up).
- **Clerics, Druids, Paladins**: Prepare a number of spells after a long rest = spellcasting ability modifier + class level (minimum 1). Can change prepared spells each long rest.
- **Wizards**: Prepare like clerics, but from their spellbook. Add 2 spells to spellbook each level for free.
