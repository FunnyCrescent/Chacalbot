# Downtime Activity Rules — D&D 5e (2014 PHB / Xanathar's Guide)

Downtime activities are performed between adventures, typically during rests or when the party is not actively4 actively engaged in active play. These rules govern how downtime affects the world and characters.

## Available Downtime Activities

### 1. Crafting

A character can craft a nonmagical item during downtime.

**Rules**:
- You must be proficient with the relevant tools (smith's tools for armor, woodcarver's tools for bows, etc.).
- **Progress**: You can craft 5 gp worth of item progress per day. Divide the item's cost in gp by 5 to determine the number of days needed.
- **Multiple crafters**: Multiple characters can collaborate, each contributing 5 gp/day, to speed up crafting.
- **Materials cost**: You must spend raw materials worth half the item's selling price.
- **Arcane/Divine crafting**: Magic item crafting is a **DM discretion** activity. Xanathar's provides optional rules:
  - Common magic item: 1 workweek (5 days) + 100 gp.
  - Uncommon: 2 workweeks + 500 gp.
  - Rare: 10 workweeks + 5,000 gp + CR 9+ creature essence.
  - Very Rare: 25 workweeks + 50,000 gp + CR 17+ creature essence.
  - Legendary: 50 workweeks + 500,000 gp + CR 21+ creature essence.

**World Effect**: Crafting creates items that enter the local economy. A blacksmith crafting weapons may attract buyers or attention from the local guard. Crafting rare items may draw curiosity from thieves or nobles.

### 2. Training

A character can learn a new language, tool proficiency, or skill (with DM approval).

**Rules** (Xanathar's):
- **Learning a language or tool**: 10 workweeks (250 days) + 25 gp for a tutor.
- **Training a skill**: Not normally possible to gain skill proficiency through downtime alone (requires feats or class features). However, DMs may allow it as a reward for extensive training (typically 250 days + 250 gp).
- **Training a weapon/armor proficiency**: DM discretion. Suggested: 250 days of training.

**World Effect**: The character must find a tutor. The tutor is an NPC who may become a recurring contact, rival, or quest hook. Training in a city may attract the attention of guilds or authorities.

### 3. Working (Earning a Living)

A character can work a profession during downtime to earn money.

**Rules**:
- Choose a profession (must have relevant tool proficiency or background).
- Make an ability check using the relevant tool or ability: **result ÷ 2 = gp earned per workweek** (rounded down).
- **Lifestyle expenses**: The character must still pay lifestyle expenses. Net earnings = gross earnings - lifestyle cost.
- **Squalid**: 1 sp/day | **Poor**: 2 sp/day | **Modest**: 1 gp/day | **Comfortable**: 2 gp/day | **Wealthy**: 4 gp/day | **Aristocratic**: 10 gp/day.

**World Effect**: Working establishes the character's presence in the community. Consistent work may lead to:
- Reputation changes with the local guild or community.
- Job offers, rumors, and plot hooks.
- Tax collectors, business rivals, or corrupt officials.
- Being recognized in the area ("the sellsword who works at the smithy").

### 4. Recuperating

A character can rest and recover from injuries or conditions.

**Rules** (Xanathar's):
- **3 days of light activity**: After 3 days, the character can make a DC 15 Constitution save. Success: end one effect that reduces hit point maximum or one disease. Can also reduce exhaustion by 1 level per day.
- **Medical care**: A character who receives care from a healer (Medicine check DC 15) gains advantage on the recuperation save.

**World Effect**: Recuperating requires a safe location. In a dangerous area, the party may be attacked while resting. Healing in a temple may incur costs or require service in return.

### 5. Carousing

A character can socialize and make connections during downtime.

**Rules** (Xanathar's):
- **Carousing DC**: Based on lifestyle. Modest/Poor: DC 15, Comfortable: DC 10, Wealthy/Aristocratic: DC 5 (easier to impress when you spend more).
- **Results** (d6):
  1. **Rival**: You make an enemy. The rival may sabotage, compete, or attack you.
  2-3. **No effect**: Pleasant time, no lasting consequences.
  4-5. **Contact**: You make a friendly contact (ally, informant, patron).
  6. **Rival + Contact**: You impress one person but offend another.

**Carousing Complications** (DM's option):
- You are accused of a crime you didn't commit.
- You get into a brawl and are arrested.
- You make a romantic enemy of a noble's spouse.
- You gamble away a significant item or gold.
- You accidentally reveal sensitive information.
- A secret society tries to recruit you.

**World Effect**: Carousing changes the social landscape. Rivals and contacts persist and affect future interactions. Carousing in a city may be noticed by factions, thieves' guilds, or nobility.

### 6. Research

A character can research lore, rumors, or information.

**Rules** (Xanathar's):
- **Cost**: 1 gp per day (for access to libraries, sages, or information brokers).
- **Time**: 1 workweek (5 days) per research attempt.
- **Check**: Intelligence (Investigation) or Intelligence (Arcana/History/Nature/Religion) DC set by DM based on obscurity.
- **Results**: Success reveals the sought information (or a lead). Failure reveals nothing or misleading information.

**World Effect**: Research may lead to quest hooks, map locations, or NPC contacts. Libraries and sages may be guarded, require introduction, or charge premium rates.

### 7. Pit Fighting / Gambling

**Rules**:
- **Pit Fighting**: Make a series of checks (STR/DEX/CON) against opponents. Prize money based on tier.
- **Gambling**: Bet gold on games of chance. Make a series of checks (usually WIS (Insight) or CHA (Deception)). **The house always has an edge.**

**World Effect**: Fighting in pits builds reputation (or infamy). Gambling debts attract dangerous collectors. Both may attract the attention of organized crime.

## Downtime and the World State

**CRITICAL RULE**: Downtime activities **do not pause the world**. While the party is doing downtime, the world continues to evolve:

1. **Time passes**: The game clock advances. Seasons change. Weather shifts.
2. **NPCs act**: NPCs have their own goals and schedules. A rival may attack while the party rests. A faction may complete its plans.
3. **Events resolve**: World events (wars, plagues, festivals) proceed without player intervention.
4. **Rumors spread**: Information the party gathered may reach their enemies.
5. **Economy fluctuates**: Supply and demand shift. Prices change.
6. **Political shifts**: Factions gain or lose power. Leaders change.

The DM should always describe what happened in the world during downtime — never treat it as a "pause button."
