# Combat Rules — D&D 5e (2014 PHB)

## Action Economy

Each creature on its turn receives:
- **1 Action** — the main activity (Attack action, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object).
- **1 Bonus Action** — only if a feature, spell, or ability explicitly grants one. You cannot trade your Action for a Bonus Action or vice versa.
- **1 Reaction** — triggered outside your turn (opportunity attack, counterspell, shield, etc.). You regain your Reaction at the start of your next turn.
- **Movement** — up to your Speed (walking, swimming, climbing, flying). Movement can be broken up across the turn. Difficult terrain costs 2 feet per 1 foot moved.
- **Free Interaction** — one object interaction per turn (draw a weapon, open a door, stow a shield). A second interaction costs your Action (Use an Object).
- **Communication** — brief speech or gestures as a free activity; cannot be a full speech.

### Two-Weapon Fighting
When you attack with a light melee weapon in your main hand, you may use a Bonus Action to attack with a different light melee weapon in your off hand. The off-hand attack does NOT add your ability modifier to damage (unless the modifier is negative).

## Attack Rolls

**Attack Roll** = d20 + Ability Modifier + Proficiency Bonus (if proficient)

- **Melee attacks**: Strength modifier by default. Finesse weapons allow Dexterity instead.
- **Ranged attacks**: Dexterity modifier. Ranged attacks at long range have disadvantage. Ranged attacks within 5 ft of a hostile creature have disadvantage (unless you have Crossbow Expert or similar).
- **Spell attacks**: Use spellcasting ability modifier (INT for wizard, CHA for sorcerer/warlock/bard, WIS for cleric/druid/ranger).

**Critical Hit**: Natural 20 on the d20. Roll damage dice twice (or double the dice). Critical hits always hit regardless of target AC. Some features expand crit range (Champion Fighter 19-20).

**Critical Miss (Natural 1)**: The attack automatically misses. No additional penalty beyond the miss (no fumble tables, no hitting allies — unless the DM rules otherwise as a house rule).

## Armor Class (AC)

**Without armor**: AC = 10 + Dexterity modifier
**With armor**:
- Light armor: AC = armor base + Dexterity modifier (full)
- Medium armor: AC = armor base + Dexterity modifier (max +2)
- Heavy armor: AC = armor base (no Dex modifier)
**With shield**: AC + 2

Special cases:
- Barbarian Unarmored Defense: AC = 10 + Dex mod + Con mod
- Monk Unarmored Defense: AC = 10 + Dex mod + Wis mod
- Mage Armor spell: AC = 13 + Dex mod
- Natural Armor: Use the formula from the race/feature (e.g., Lizardfolk AC = 13 + Dex mod)

## Damage

**Damage Rolls**: Roll the weapon/spell dice + ability modifier + any bonuses.
- Damage types: bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, thunder.
- Resistance: half damage. Vulnerability: double damage. Immunity: no damage.
- Multiple resistances/vulnerabilities do not stack — apply only once per damage type.
- Damage minimum is 1 (after resistance/immunity calculations, a hit always deals at least 1 damage unless the target is immune).

## Death Saves

When HP drops to 0, the creature falls unconscious and begins making death saving throws:
- **Death Save**: d20 roll, no modifiers.
- **Success**: Natural 10-19 = 1 success. Natural 20 = regain 1 HP and regain consciousness.
- **Failure**: Natural 1-9 = 1 failure. Natural 1 = 2 failures.
- **Death**: 3 failures = death. **Stabilization**: 3 successes = stable (0 HP, unconscious, but no longer making saves).
- Taking damage while at 0 HP: any damage = 1 failure. Critical hit while at 0 HP = 2 failures.
- Healing any amount restores consciousness at the HP gained.

## Conditions

Key conditions and their effects:
- **Blinded**: Auto-fail sight checks; attacks have disadvantage; attacks against you have advantage.
- **Charmed**: Cannot attack the charmer; charmer has advantage on social checks.
- **Concentrating**: If damaged, must make CON save (DC = 10 or half damage, whichever is higher) or lose concentration.
- **Frightened**: Disadvantage on checks/attacks while source is in sight; cannot willingly move closer.
- **Grappled**: Speed becomes 0. Ends if grappler is incapacitated or effect removes the grappled creature.
- **Incapacitated**: No actions or reactions.
- **Invisible**: Impossible to see without special sense; attacks have advantage; attacks against have disadvantage.
- **Paralyzed**: Incapacitated + auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks within 5 ft have advantage and are automatic crits.
- **Petrified**: Incapacitated + resistant to all damage + auto-fail STR/DEX saves.
- **Poisoned**: Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
- **Prone**: Disadvantage on attacks; melee attacks against have advantage; ranged attacks against have disadvantage; movement costs extra to stand up.
- **Restrained**: Speed 0; attacks have disadvantage; attacks against have advantage; disadvantage on Dex saves.
- **Stunned**: Incapacitated + cannot move; attacks against have advantage; auto-fail STR/DEX saves.
- **Unconscious**: Incapacitated + cannot move/speak; attacks within 5 ft auto-crit; attacks against have advantage; drops everything held; falls prone.
