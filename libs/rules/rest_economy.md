# Rest Economy & Resource Recovery — D&D 5e (2014 PHB)

## Short Rest

A **Short Rest** is a period of downtime, at least **1 hour** long, during which a character does nothing more strenuous than light activity: reading, talking, eating, standing watch, resting.

### What Recovers on a Short Rest
- **Hit Dice**: A character can spend one or more Hit Dice to regain hit points. Roll each hit die and add the Constitution modifier to the result (minimum 1 HP per die). The player chooses how many dice to roll (up to their remaining unspent hit dice).
- **Warlock Spell Slots**: Pact Magic slots recover on a short rest.
- **Class Features (short-rest)**: Features that specify recovery on a short rest:
  - Fighter's **Action Surge** (levels 2+): Recovers after a short rest (or at level 17+, after 1 minute).
  - Fighter's **Second Wind**: Recovers after a short rest.
  - Monk's **Ki**: Fully recovers after a short rest.
  - Bard's **Song of Rest** (die roll): Available again.
  - Druid's **Wild Shape** (Circle of the Moon): Uses recover on a short rest.
  - Barbarian's **Rage**: NOT recovered on short rest (long rest only).

### Limitations
- A character cannot spend more Hit Dice than they have available. Hit Dice are tracked per class for multiclass characters.
- Armor cannot be donned or doffed during a short rest (donning takes 5-10 minutes, but is considered "more strenuous" by many tables — DM discretion).
- A short rest is interrupted by combat or strenuous activity (running, fighting, spellcasting). The rest must restart.

## Long Rest

A **Long Rest** is a period of extended downtime, at least **8 hours** long, during which a character sleeps for at least 6 hours and performs no more than 2 hours of light activity (keeping watch, reading, talking, eating).

### What Recovers on a Long Rest
- **Hit Points**: All hit points are restored.
- **Hit Dice**: Recover up to half the character's total Hit Dice (rounded down, minimum 1). Example: A level 7 Fighter has 7d10 hit dice. After a long rest, they recover 3d10 (7÷2 = 3.5, rounded down).
- **Spell Slots**: All expended spell slots are restored (for all classes except Warlock, whose pact slots recover on short rest — but they also recover on long rest).
- **All Class Features**: Features that recover on a long rest:
  - Barbarian's **Rage uses**: All restored.
  - Bard's **Bardic Inspiration** uses: All restored.
  - Cleric's **Channel Divinity**: 1 use restored (or more at higher levels).
  - Druid's **Wild Shape uses**: All restored.
  - Fighter's **Action Surge**, **Indomitable** (level 9+): Restored.
  - Paladin's **Divine Smite**, **Lay on Hands**, **Channel Divinity**: Restored.
  - Ranger's **Favored Enemy** abilities, spell slots: Restored.
  - Rogue's **Cunning Action**: Always available (not a limited resource).
  - Sorcerer's **Sorcery Points**, **Metamagic**: Restored.
  - Wizard's **Arcane Recovery** (once per long rest): Available again.

### Long Rest Interruption
- If the rest is interrupted by a period of strenuous activity (at least 1 hour of walking, fighting, casting spells, or similar), the characters gain **no benefit** from the rest and must restart the 8-hour period.
- A single combat encounter (typically less than 1 minute) does NOT interrupt a long rest — it's the 1-hour threshold of strenuous activity that matters.

### Long Rest Limitations (Xanathar's / Common Rulings)
- A character can benefit from no more than one long rest in a 24-hour period.
- A character who has 0 hit points cannot take a long rest until they have at least 1 hit point (usually through stabilization + natural recovery, or healing magic).

## Hit Dice Tracking

Each class grants hit dice per level:
| Class     | Hit Die | Class     | Hit Die |
|-----------|---------|-----------|---------|
| Barbarian | d12     | Fighter   | d10     |
| Bard      | d8      | Monk      | d8      |
| Cleric    | d8      | Paladin   | d10     |
| Druid     | d8      | Ranger    | d10     |
| Rogue     | d8      | Sorcerer  | d6      |
| Warlock   | d8      | Wizard    | d6      |

**Multiclass hit dice**: Tracked separately per class. A Fighter 4 / Rogue 3 has 4d10 + 3d8 hit dice. On a long rest, they recover 2d10 + 1d8 (half of each, rounded down individually).

## Natural Healing (Variant)

**DM Option — Healing Surge**: At the end of a long rest, characters may choose to spend hit dice to heal before the long rest's full HP recovery. In the standard rules, a long rest restores all HP — but some tables use the variant where a long rest only recovers hit dice worth of HP (like a more powerful short rest).

## Resource Recovery Summary

| Resource         | Short Rest | Long Rest              |
|------------------|------------|------------------------|
| HP               | Spend HD   | Full restore           |
| Hit Dice         | Spend HD   | Recover half total     |
| Spell Slots      | Warlock only| All restored           |
| Rage             | No         | All restored           |
| Ki               | All        | All restored           |
| Action Surge     | Yes        | Yes                    |
| Bardic Insp.     | No         | All restored           |
| Channel Divinity | No         | 1+ uses restored       |
| Sorcery Points   | No         | All restored           |
